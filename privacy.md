# Hold Close Privacy Policy

_Effective: October 31, 2026_

Hold Close helps you keep track of when you last talked to the people you care about. It was built so that your information never leaves your phone.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in.
- No servers. We don't collect, store, or receive any of your data.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- Everything Hold Close knows is stored only on your iPhone, and you can delete it at any time.

## What Hold Close uses, and why

**Contacts you choose.** You add people using Apple's contact picker, so Hold Close only receives the contacts you pick: their name, phone numbers, and email addresses. It does not have access to the rest of your address book.

**Your check-in history.** Hold Close keeps a record of when you were in touch with each person (for example "Texted, October 12") and how often you'd like to be in touch. This is stored on your device.

**Calendar (optional).** If you choose to connect your calendar, Hold Close reads past events on your device to find ones that include someone you've added, by their email address or full name. It saves only the date of a matching event, not its title, notes, location, or other attendees. Calendar data is never sent anywhere. You can turn this off any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

**Texts you receive (optional).** iOS does not let apps read your messages. If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation, iOS passes Hold Close only the sender's name, phone number, or email so it can log that you were in touch. Hold Close never receives the content of any message.

**Texts you send from the app.** When you text someone using the in-app Messages composer, iOS tells Hold Close only whether the message was sent, so it can log it. Hold Close does not see or store what you wrote.

**Calls.** Apps can't see your calls. After you call someone from Hold Close, it simply asks whether you connected.

**Notifications (optional).** Weekly nudges and follow-up reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification passes through a server.

**Widget.** The home screen widget reads the same on-device data to show who's worth a hello.

**Clipboard.** If you choose a suggested opener for an app that can't be pre-filled (like Signal or Telegram), Hold Close copies that text to your clipboard so you can paste it. It never reads your clipboard.

## Other apps and websites

When you tap Text or Call, Hold Close opens the app you chose (Messages, WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Phone, or FaceTime) with that person's number. When you tap a support link, it opens 988lifeline.org or findahelpline.com in your browser. What happens there is covered by those services' own privacy policies.

## Backups

Hold Close data is stored like any other app's data on your iPhone. If you back up your phone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, your Hold Close data is included in that backup, handled by Apple under its privacy policy. We have no access to it.

## Deleting your data

Swipe to delete any person or history entry in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.

## Children

Hold Close is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. Because Hold Close doesn't collect data, any change would be about how the app works on your device.
