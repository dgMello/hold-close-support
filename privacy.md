# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Your privacy matters. Hold Close is built so that your personal information stays yours.

## Information We Collect

None. Hold Close does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Contacts Access

Hold Close asks for permission to access your contacts so the app can work with the people you choose. Your contacts are used only within the app on your device. They are never uploaded, collected, or shared with us or anyone else.

Contacts access is optional, and you can turn it on or off at any time in your device's Settings.

## Third Parties

Because we don't collect your personal data, we have nothing to sell, rent, or share with advertisers, analytics providers, or anyone else.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
